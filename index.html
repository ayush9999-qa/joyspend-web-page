Privacy is Our Priority

01.

No Data Selling

We believe your financial life is private. JoySpend will never sell your personal data or spending habits to advertisers. Period.

02.

Secure Cloud Protection

All data synced to the cloud is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard AES-256 protocols. Your "Joy Scores" and transaction notes remain private and secure on Google Cloud's infrastructure.

03.

Seamless Multi-Device Access

Log an expense on one device and see it on another. Your data syncs securely across all your devices, and you can delete your entire account and all associated data permanently at any time.
