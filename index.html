Understand Your Data Choices

01.

Local Core Tracking

Core transactions are stored on your device. You can use manual tracking without an account. Technical diagnostics may still be processed by Firebase Crashlytics.

02.

Optional Cloud Sync

Signing in enables cloud sync of transactions and recurring records through Google Firebase. Synced records include amounts, notes and emotion tags. Internet access is required.

03.

Optional AI Processing

When you use AI, your questions and relevant spending context are processed by JoySpend’s online service and Google AI. Subscription services use RevenueCat and your app store. AI answers can be wrong; use them to support your own decisions.
